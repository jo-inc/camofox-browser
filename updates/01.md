# Update 01: pop-ups, page state and macros

## What needed adding or fixing

1. **The agent cannot tell what kind of page it is on.** A captcha, a sign-in wall, a cookie banner and an empty result set all look the same to it, so it wastes steps guessing.
2. **Pop-ups block the agent, and there is no safe way to close them.** Clicking the wrong button could accept terms or submit a form.
3. **Search coverage is thin.** Only 14 macros, mostly general or social sites, and none for jobs, housing, research or shopping beyond Amazon.

## What was added

1. **Page-state route, `GET /tabs/:tabId/state`.** Returns the URL, title, a classification (captcha, block, two-factor, login wall, consent, no results, or ok), and the refs of overlays that can be closed.
2. **Overlay dismissal route, `POST /tabs/:tabId/overlays/dismiss`.** Clicks only exact close labels on buttons, never accept, sign-in, buy, submit or continue controls. Capped at five clicks and reports what it could not close.
3. **Four verified macros.** `@bestbuy_search`, `@indeed_search`, `@pubmed_search` and `@zillow_search`, with unit tests.

## Not done in this update

- Stealth benchmark script (benchmark pages still to be chosen).
- UK and other-country macros (not confirmed from this environment).
- Eve tool wrappers for the two new routes.
- Live run on Railway.

See `docs/browser-experience-plan.md` for the full plan and the decisions still open.
